# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE:23-10-2026

## AIM
To create the solid model from given drawing and section the solid model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:

Step 1: Study the given drawing

1. Carefully examine the isometric view to understand the
overall shape of the component.
2. Identify the different features:
○ A flat base plate with rounded ends.<br>
○ A raised cylindrical boss at the centre.<br>
○ A central through-hole.<br>
○ A counterbore at the top of the central hole.<br>
○ Two mounting holes on the base.<br>
3. Study the top view, front view, and sectional view to
understand the dimensions and relative positions of all
features.
4. Identify the dimensions given in the drawing and select
a suitable scale.

<img width="385" height="617" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8152e88-52c7-4d96-81d3-4a25375632bf" />

Step 2: Prepare the drawing sheet

1. Fix the drawing sheet properly on the drawing board.
2. Draw the border and title block according to the
prescribed engineering drawing format.
3. Select a suitable position for the orthographic views and
the isometric view.
4. Draw light horizontal and vertical reference lines using a
T-square or mini drafter.
5. Maintain sufficient spacing between views for
dimensioning.

<img width="707" height="345" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5284d58a-6010-48a1-bd3c-f8c61be3368a" />

Step 3: Draw the top view

1. Draw the horizontal and vertical centre lines of the
component.
2. Draw the outer profile of the base plate, including its
rounded ends and curved edges.
3. Mark the centre of the raised cylindrical boss.
4. Draw the concentric circles representing the boss and
central hole:
○ Outer diameter of the boss: Ø50 mm.<br>
○ Central bore diameter: Ø20 mm.<br>
○ Counterbore diameter: Ø30 mm.<br>
5. Locate the two mounting holes on the base using the
given dimensions.
6. Draw both mounting holes with a diameter of Ø12 mm.
7. Draw the required centre lines through the circular
features.
8. Check the alignment and symmetry of the entire top
view.

<img width="545" height="273" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b4e9ccb-0bb9-4ad5-bad4-77005ed72f25" />

Step 4: Draw the front view

1. Project vertical lines downward or upward from the top
view, as required by the selected projection method.
2. Draw the base plate with a thickness of 10 mm.
3. Draw the raised cylindrical boss above the base.
4. Mark the height of the boss as 28 mm above the base
plate.
5. Draw the upper counterbore and central bore according
to the sectional dimensions.
6. Represent the mounting holes using hidden lines
wherever their edges are not directly visible.
7. Draw the centre lines of the boss and holes.
8. Darken the visible outlines after checking all
dimensions.

<img width="531" height="633" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/52882d6d-9467-424f-beb3-ed68e94987a4" />

Step 5: Draw the sectional view

1. Identify the cutting plane shown in the given drawing.
2. Draw the sectional view by projecting the corresponding
features from the other views.
3. Represent the central bore and counterbore accurately.
4. Draw the counterbore with a diameter of Ø30 mm and a
depth of 6 mm.
5. Draw the central through-hole with a diameter of Ø20
mm.
6. Represent the mounting holes and their locations in the
base plate.
7. Apply uniform section hatching to the solid material cut
by the section plane.
8. Do not hatch the holes, bores, or empty spaces.
9. Use thin lines for hatching and thicker lines for visible
outlines.

Step 6: Draw the isometric view

1. Draw the isometric axes: one vertical axis and two axes
inclined at 30° to the horizontal.
2. Construct the base plate in isometric form using the
dimensions from the orthographic views.
3. Draw the rounded ends and curved edges of the base
plate.
4. Construct the raised cylindrical boss on the base.
5. Represent the circular top surfaces as isometric
ellipses.
6. Draw the central bore and counterbore as concentric
isometric ellipses.
7. Show the two mounting holes on the base plate.
8. Draw the visible edges of the component using
continuous thick lines.
9. Remove unwanted construction lines and complete the
isometric representation.

Step 7: Dimensioning and finishing

1. Add the overall dimensions and the dimensions of the
individual features.
2. Indicate the diameters of all circular features using the
Ø symbol.
3. Mark the radius of the rounded ends using the R
symbol.
4. Add the centre lines and hidden lines wherever
necessary.
5. Ensure that the dimensions are clear and do not
overlap.
6. Check that the top, front, sectional, and isometric views
agree with one another.
7. Darken the final outlines and erase unnecessary
construction lines.
8. Complete the title block with the drawing title, scale,
date, and other required details.

Important: Some small dimensions in the supplied image
are difficult to read clearly. Before preparing the final
drawing, verify the exact mounting-hole spacing,
counterbore depth, and base dimensions from the original
drawing or your instructor's specification.
